# Code of Conduct

foma-skills is a small open-source project. Be the kind of collaborator you would want to work with.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific and constructive: say what you saw, what you expected, and how to reproduce it.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Accept that this is a one-maintainer project: replies can take a few days.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or pushing a third-party skill or service in issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people talk about the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If something crosses the line, open an issue for public matters, or contact the maintainer through the contact options on their GitHub profile for private ones. Security problems go through the private reporting described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this document.
