# Engineering notes

Why the pack looks the way it does, and the evidence behind its claims.

- [`decisions.md`](decisions.md): the decision log, one `DEC-NNN` entry per decision, newest last.
- [`test-ledger.md`](test-ledger.md): what was tested, what failed, and which prompts or checklists were replaced after a failure.

IDs such as `SRC-`, `FACT-`, `CAND-`, `IDEA-` and `Q-` point to the maintainer's private research notes and do not resolve here; the `DEC-` entries are complete on their own. Test numbers come from small samples and are indicative, not proven.
